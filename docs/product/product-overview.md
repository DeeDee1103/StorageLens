# Product Overview

## Product Vision

StorageLens provides enterprise visibility into distributed storage landscapes and enables data reduction decisions through analytics and duplicate intelligence.

## Target Stakeholders

- Infrastructure and storage teams
- IT operations
- Governance and compliance teams
- Product and engineering leadership

## Value Proposition

- Faster discovery of storage inefficiencies
- Workflow-driven scan operations
- Actionable duplicate insights for cost optimization

## Current Product Scope

- End-to-end scan orchestration
- Metadata inventory and analytics
- Duplicate detection and reporting
